How it works

Three seconds from shelf to answer.

01

Scan

Point your camera at the barcode, or type the number in. ScanScore checks three open product databases at once.

02

Score

You get a number from 0 to 100, and a chart showing how every factor pushed it up or down.

03

Understand

Tap any ingredient for a plain-English explanation. See what's flagged, and why it matters.

The scale

One number. Four clear verdicts.

Good75–100Buy with confidence
Okay50–74Fine, with caveats
Poor25–49Look for better
Bad0–24Skip it
